# SIH 2026 — Slide 1 & 2: Problem Statement & Context

## Problem Statement: PS-04
**Space Debris Tracking & Satellite Collision Risk Prediction Dashboard**

### The Escalating Orbital Crisis
1. **Exponential Low Earth Orbit (LEO) Congestion:**
   - 40,000+ tracked space objects (operational satellites, discarded rocket stages, spent fuel tanks).
   - Millions of lethal millimetre-to-centimetre debris fragments traveling at relative speeds up to $15\text{ km/s}$.
2. **Kessler Syndrome Threat:**
   - A single catastrophic collision cascading into thousands of fragments, rendering key orbital altitudes unusable for generations.
3. **Accessibility Gap in Current SSA Solutions:**
   - Proprietary military/enterprise SSA networks are expensive and inaccessible to small satellite operators, academic institutions, and emerging space startups.
   - Lack of explainable, deterministic screening tools for rapid decision-making.
